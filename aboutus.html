About Us

Our floral shop is a modern floral studio
born from a weekend hobby and a serious love for fresh flowers.
What started as market-day arrangements
quickly grew into a full-time passion for creating beautiful, intentional floral designs.
We focus on seasonal blooms, clean lines, and a less-is-more approach.
From everyday bouquets to wedding installs,
our style is modern, unfussy, and made to leave a lasting impression.
